R&D Tax Credits

R&D tax relief is one of the most generous incentives available to UK businesses — yet thousands of eligible companies fail to claim it each year. If your business is developing new products, processes, software or systems, you likely qualify.

Our specialists identify eligible R&D activity across your business, prepare robust technical and financial narratives, and submit claims that stand up to HMRC scrutiny — maximising your relief while minimising risk.

  • Eligibility assessment (free)
  • Technical & financial narrative preparation
  • HMRC submission & correspondence
  • Ongoing R&D tracking strategy

VAT & Making Tax Digital

VAT is one of the most complex areas of UK tax — and one of the most expensive to get wrong. Our VAT specialists handle everything from registration and returns to partial exemption calculations, ensuring accuracy and full MTD compliance.

With Making Tax Digital now mandatory for all VAT-registered businesses, we ensure your software and processes are fully compliant — and we proactively identify opportunities to optimise your VAT position.

  • VAT registration & deregistration
  • Quarterly VAT returns & filing
  • Making Tax Digital setup & compliance
  • Partial exemption & mixed supply VAT
  • International & cross-border VAT
