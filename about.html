Our Story

Built on a belief that businesses deserve better financial advice.

Account Clerks was founded in 2005 by James Whitfield, a Chartered Accountant who had spent a decade in Big Four firms before realising that the UK's small and medium businesses were being underserved. They paid for compliance, but rarely received the strategic guidance that could genuinely change their trajectory.

Starting with 10 local clients in Welwyn Garden City, James built a practice around a simple principle: every client gets the attention, insight and proactive advice that makes a real difference to their business.

Today, Account Clerks employs 12 qualified accountants and advisors, serves 500+ businesses across the UK, and maintains a 98% client retention rate — the clearest indicator of genuine value delivered. We are ICAEW registered, ACCA members, and Xero Gold Partners.
