The Process

Our 5-step switch process.

We've refined this process across hundreds of client transitions. It takes 5–10 working days and requires minimal effort from you. We do the heavy lifting.

Start Today
01

Free Discovery Call

30 minutes, no commitment. We learn about your business and current situation. You decide if we're the right fit — no pressure.

02

Professional Clearance

We issue a professional clearance letter to your previous accountant on your behalf. This is standard practice and they are legally obliged to respond.

03

File Transfer & HMRC Auth

We collect all historic records, prior year accounts, payroll data, and HMRC authorisation codes. You don't need to chase your previous accountant.

04

Free Financial Health Review

We complete a comprehensive review of your financial position, identifying any missed opportunities, compliance gaps, or areas for immediate improvement — at no charge.

Active Partnership Begins

You're introduced to your dedicated account manager. Onboarding is complete. From day one, we're actively managing your accounts and looking for opportunities.

Full Transfer

Everything transferred. Nothing left behind.

We ensure a complete handover of all records and authorisations. You'll never have to chase your old accountant for a document again.
