Retail & E-commerce

Retail and e-commerce businesses face a unique set of financial challenges — fluctuating stock levels, complex VAT across different product types, marketplace fees, and the pressure of thin margins. Add cross-border selling and the picture becomes even more complicated.

We understand the retail P&L inside out. From optimising your VAT position on mixed supplies to reconciling marketplace settlements from Amazon, eBay and Shopify, we make sure your numbers are accurate and your tax position is optimised.

  • Stock valuation & cost of goods
  • Marketplace (Amazon/eBay) reconciliation
  • VAT on mixed supplies & digital goods
  • Cross-border & EU VAT (IOSS/OSS)
  • Shopify/WooCommerce integration
Talk to a retail specialist

Key challenge

VAT on mixed supplies

Different VAT rates apply to different product types. We ensure your VAT is correct across every category — avoiding costly errors.

Construction & Trades

Construction businesses operate under a unique tax framework — the Construction Industry Scheme (CIS) adds significant complexity to payroll, subcontractor management, and cash flow. The domestic reverse charge VAT rules add another layer.

We are specialists in CIS compliance — verifying subcontractors, making correct deductions, filing monthly returns, and maximising CIS refunds. We also navigate the domestic reverse charge VAT rules, ensuring your invoicing and returns are always correct.

  • CIS monthly returns & verification
  • Domestic reverse charge VAT
  • Subcontractor payroll & deductions
  • CIS tax refund claims
  • Project-based P&L reporting
Talk to a construction specialist

Key challenge

CIS compliance

Non-compliance with CIS can result in significant HMRC penalties. We manage every aspect of your CIS obligations.

Tech & SaaS

We work with early-stage startups through to Series B companies, providing the financial infrastructure needed to scale — investor-grade reporting, cap table modelling, and R&D claims that maximise your credit without HMRC scrutiny.

  • R&D tax credits (SME & RDEC)
  • EMI share option schemes
  • SEIS/EIS investor relief setup
  • Investor reporting & board packs
  • SaaS MRR/ARR financial modelling

Nurseries & Childcare

Day nurseries and childcare providers operate in a uniquely complex VAT environment. Government funding, exempt supplies, and mixed-rate services create specific challenges that require specialist knowledge — and getting it wrong is costly.

We are sector specialists with extensive experience in childcare finance — from VAT exemption analysis and funding reconciliation to Ofsted financial reporting and staff:child ratio cost modelling.

  • VAT exemption & partial exemption
  • EYPP/Free hours funding reconciliation
  • Ofsted financial reporting
  • Staff cost & ratio modelling
Talk to a childcare specialist

Sector insight

VAT on childcare

Most childcare is VAT-exempt, but mixed supplies (meals, activities) can create complex partial exemption calculations. We navigate this for you.

Healthcare & Wellness

Healthcare professionals — from NHS contractors and GPs to private practitioners and wellness clinics — face a distinctive mix of regulatory requirements and tax considerations. The NHS pension, IR35 for locums, and VAT on medical services all require careful handling.

We support NHS contractors, private clinics, dental practices, physios, and wellness businesses with specialist accounting that understands the sector's unique financial landscape.

  • NHS contractor accounts & tax
  • Private practice bookkeeping
  • NHS pension annual allowance
  • IR35 & locum tax advice

NHS pension trap

Many NHS professionals face unexpected annual allowance charges. We proactively plan to minimise your pension tax exposure.

Property & Real Estate

Property is one of the most tax-intensive asset classes in the UK. From SDLT planning and CGT on disposals to the structure of your property portfolio — the right advice at the right time can save tens of thousands of pounds.

We advise landlords, developers and property investors on the most tax-efficient structure for their portfolios, whether that means holding property personally, in a limited company, or through a trust.

  • BTL landlord accounts & tax returns
  • CGT planning & calculations
  • SDLT advice & structure planning
  • Ltd company vs personal ownership

Hospitality & Food

Restaurants, cafes, bars, and food businesses operate on thin margins with complex VAT rules, significant cash flow challenges, and the added complexity of tips, tronc arrangements, and seasonal income fluctuations.

We understand the hospitality sector's pressures — from managing the VAT on food versus drinks to setting up compliant tronc arrangements for staff tips that minimise NIC exposure.

  • Cash flow & weekly P&L monitoring
  • Food & drink VAT (split supply)
  • Tronc & tips payroll setup
  • Seasonal forecasting & budgeting
